The First Missionary Journey

    Travel times assume about 20 miles (30 km) a day on foot and 75 miles (120 km) a day under sail with a fair wind. They cover time on the move only. Acts gives no dates and few durations for the stays; the whole journey is usually dated to about AD 46–48 and reckoned at one to two years.

    Roads and sea lanes are drawn as plausible lines between the places Acts names, so distances are approximate. The sites of Lystra and Derbe are identified from inscriptions and are less certain than the others. Coastline from Natural Earth.
